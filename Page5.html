1. Misinformation and Disinformation

Misinformation and disinformation are common problems in the digital age. False or misleading information can spread quickly through social media and may affect people's decisions and understanding of important issues. Rizal’s emphasis on education, knowledge, and critical thinking provides an important lesson for this problem. Instead of immediately believing or sharing information, people should examine its source, compare it with reliable references, and determine whether theinformation is supported by evidence.

1. Misinformation and Disinformation

Rizal’s insight:

Education should help people become thoughtful and informed citizens who can distinguish truth from false information.

Application today:

Students can practice responsible digital citizenship by checking sources before sharing posts, avoiding unverified claims, and correcting false information respectfully

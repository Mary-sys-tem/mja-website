Rizal's Life

1.Childhood and Family

• José Protasio Rizal Mercado y Alonso Realonda was born on June 19, 1861, in Calamba, Laguna.

• He was the seventh of eleven children of Francisco Mercado and Teodora Alonso Realonda. Rizal grew up in an educated and hardworking family.

• His mother was one of his first teachers and encouraged his love for reading, writing, and learning.

• His family played an important role in developing his character, intelligence, and love for his country.

2.Education

• Rizal received his early education at home, where his mother taught him reading, writing, and basic lessons. He later studied at the Ateneo Municipal de Manila, where he earned a Bachelor of Arts degree.

• He continued his studies at the University of Santo Tomas and later traveled to Spain to study at the Universidad Central de Madrid. He studied medicine and specialized in ophthalmology.

• Rizal also learned several languages and developed interests in literature, science, history, and the arts.

3.Travels

• Rizal traveled to different countries to continue his education, study different cultures, and observe how other societies developed.

• He traveled through Singapore, Spain, France, Germany, Italy, Austria, Switzerland, Belgium, Japan, Hong Kong, and other places.

• His travels exposed him to new ideas about education, government, science, freedom, and society.

• These experiences influenced his writings and helped him develop his ideas about improving the Philippines.

4. Major Life Events

• Some important events in Rizal’s life include:

• 1861 – Born in Calamba, Laguna.

• 1877 – Graduated with a Bachelor of Arts degree from Ateneo Municipal de Manila.

• 1882 – Traveled to Spain to continue his studies.

• 1887 – Published Noli Me Tangere.

• 1891 – Published El Filibusterismo.

• 1892 – Returned to the Philippines and founded La Liga Filipina.

• 1892 – Exiled to Dapitan.

• 1896 – Arrested, tried, and executed at Bagumbayan on December 30, 1896.

2. Social Inequality and Access to Education

Social inequality continues to affect many Filipinos, particularly those who have limited access to quality education, technology, employment opportunities, and other resources. Education can play an important role in helping people improve their lives and participate in society.

Rizal strongly valued education as a means of personal and national development. His life demonstrates his belief that learning can empower people and help them understand social problems

2. Social Inequality and Access to Education

Rizal’s insight:

A society becomes stronger when its people have opportunities to learn, develop their abilities, and participate meaningfully in the community.

Application today:

Students can support equal educational opportunities, respect classmates from different backgrounds, share knowledge, and participate in community activities that help other learners.

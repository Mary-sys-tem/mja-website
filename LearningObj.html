Learning Objectives:

At the end of the activity, students should be able to:

1.Explain significant events, contributions, writings, and achievements of Dr. José Rizal and demonstrate their importance to Philippine history and nation-building;

2.Research, evaluate, and organize reliable information about Rizal’s life and works using books, scholarly references, journals, credible websites, and other authoritative sources;

3. Analyze selected writings, ideas, and principles of Rizal and establish meaningful connections between his ideas and contemporary Philippine social issues;

4.Design and develop an informative, functional, and engaging digital learning website using appropriate webdevelopment or website-building tools;

5.Demonstrate digital and media literacy by evaluating information, properly citing sources identifying misinformation or disinformation, and presenting historically accurate content; and

6.Reflect on the relevance of Rizal’s ideals, particularly education, critical thinking,patriotism, civic responsibility, and social awareness, in addressing contemporarychallenges in Philippine society
